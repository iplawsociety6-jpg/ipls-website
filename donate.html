Donate & Support

Empowering Student Creators and Future Advocates

Support the Intellectual Property Law Society

The Intellectual Property Law Society (IPLS) at Oklahoma City University School of Law is a student-led organization committed to preparing future advocates for high-stakes practice in patent, trademark, copyright, trade secret, sports, media, and entertainment law.

Your contribution supports IPLS student programming and educational opportunities, including moot court competition expenses, attorney-led educational events, and networking opportunities. Contributions will be allocated according to applicable university and student-organization policies. organization’s approved funding procedures.

Designation Pathways & Impact Areas

Donors may designate their contribution toward general society operations or direct their support toward a specific purpose or event:

General Society Support

Unrestricted operational funding that sustains year-round society programming:

  • Guest speaker hospitality and lunch-and-learn panel sessions
  • Student educational workshops and career preparation materials
  • Campus outreach and IP career networking resources
  • Society meeting operational costs and administrative supplies

Moot Court Competition Fund

Directly underwrites travel, accommodations, and entry fees for OCU Law student teams:

  • AIPLA Giles Sutherland Rich Memorial: National patent law competition
  • INTA Saul Lefkowitz: Trademark and unfair competition moot court
  • IPLAC Judicial Reception & Moot Court: James L. Watson competition
  • NAPABA Thomas Tang: National appellate advocacy competition
  • USPTO PTAB Moot Court: National patent trial and appeal competition

Event Sponsorships & Mixers

Sponsor specific current, upcoming, and future signature networking events:

  • Bowling Mixer at Dust Bowl Lanes & Lounge: Annual networking gathering with local IP attorneys
  • “Thunder Up” Attorney Panel: OKC Thunder sports, entertainment, and media legal panel
  • Dunlap Codding Networking Mixer: Spring 2027 firm reception
  • Oklahoma IP Bar Association Reception: Statewide practitioner networking
